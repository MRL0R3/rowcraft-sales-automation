# CSV and calculation contract

Required exact headers: `order_id,date,product,quantity,unit_price,status,currency`. Header order is flexible; duplicate or missing headers are errors. Additional named columns are accepted but do not affect calculations or exports; extra values without a header are errors. UTF-8 (with optional BOM), quoted commas, quoted newlines, CRLF, and blank rows are supported. Row errors show the physical CSV line where a record ends (so a quoted multiline field points to its closing line). Dates are calendar dates `YYYY-MM-DD` with no timezone. `unit_price` is a non-negative decimal with at most two fraction digits; quantity is a positive integer up to 900,719,925,474. To keep browser calculations exact, each line amount is capped at $9,007,199,254.74 (or the equivalent currency amount), which also keeps totals across 10,000 rows within safe integer cents. Currency is a three-letter uppercase code; one currency per report is required. The browser accepts currencies recognized by `Intl` with exactly two minor-unit digits; zero- or three-decimal currencies are outside this demo contract.

`completed`, `refunded`, and `cancelled` are accepted statuses. Each line amount is quantity times per-unit price. Completed revenue sums completed lines. Refunded amount sums explicit, positive `refunded` lines and is not inferred from completed rows. Net revenue is completed revenue minus refunded amount. Completed orders count unique IDs with a completed line. Average order value is completed revenue divided by unique completed orders, or `0.00` when none exist. Refund rate by value is refunded amount divided by completed revenue, or unavailable (`N/A` / `null`) when completed revenue is zero. Monthly metrics use the date on each row; refunds are assigned to their refund row's month. Top products and units include completed lines only.

Identical rows are flagged by physical CSV row number, retained, and included in totals. The parser cannot know whether repeated lines are legitimate split shipments or accidental duplicates, so review is explicit rather than silently deleting revenue. Spreadsheet exports prefix formula-leading text with an apostrophe. The browser limit is 5 MB and 10,000 rows. Python uses the same limits. No uploaded customer data is included in fixtures; the bundled data is synthetic.

Browser date, status, and product filters recalculate the report and its JSON/monthly CSV exports. Search filters only the visible line-item table. Duplicate-row warnings refer to the entire source CSV, including rows currently outside the filters.

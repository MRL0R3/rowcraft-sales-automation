# Thoughtful outreach copy

Personalize each note after researching the recipient. Do not bulk-send, claim prior results, or imply a relationship that does not exist.

## First call and discovery checklist

Open with: “Could you walk me through how the source file becomes the report you need today?” Then ask about format, representative edge cases, output, recurrence, volume, currency/date rules, refunds/cancellations, privacy and retention, hosting/ownership, access, acceptance, timeline, and support. Agree on whether sample data can be synthetic or redacted. Do not request credentials during discovery.

## Respectful follow-up sequence

1. After 4–5 business days: one short note asking whether the reporting issue is still relevant.
2. After another 7–10 business days: one final note with a useful clarification or demo link.
3. Stop if there is no reply or the person declines. No automated follow-up sequence.
